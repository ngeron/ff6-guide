# Part 1: The World of Balance

## The raid on Narshe
@guide 4.1.1
@where Narshe

Three Imperial soldiers in Magitek armor march on the mining town of Narshe to seize a frozen esper. One of them is a young woman with no name yet.

### Where to go
- Walk north through Narshe. The Magitek armor's beam attacks and Tek Missile win every fight; the woman can also heal with Heal Force.
- A few town fights are optional: an extra one by turning right at the inn, and two more if you step on the middle tile of the crossroads.
- Enter the mines. Save at the glowing point, then break through to the boss.
- After the boss, keep north to the frozen esper. The scene that follows ends with the woman waking up in an old man's house. Her name is Terra.

### Boss: [[Ymir]]
- Hit the **head**, not the shell. The head pulls back about every other turn; anything that lands on the shell gets a Mega Volt counter.
- Tek Missile is the strongest attack. A thunder beam on the shell doesn't provoke it, so it's safe filler while you wait.
- Heal Force between head appearances.

### Monsters here
[[Guard]] · [[Silver Lobo]] · [[Megalodoth]] · [[Wererat]] · [[Bandit]] · [[Spritzer]]

### Don't miss
- An **Elixir** in the clock near the old man's door.

## Escape from Narshe
@guide 4.2.1
@where Narshe

Narshe's guards want Terra. The old man sends her west through the mines, and help arrives in the form of a treasure hunter named Locke and a band of Moogles.

### Where to go
- Cross the bridge behind the old man's house into the west mine. Terra is alone: Fire ends fights quickly, and a Sleeping Bag restores MP.
- Follow the right branch north until the guards corner Terra. The scene switches to Locke.
- With Locke and the Moogles you control **three parties** (switch between them outside battle). Block the paths so the wandering monsters don't reach Terra, then defeat the guards' leader to finish.
- Leave town to the south once you're done.

### Boss: [[Guard Leader]]
- Kill his two Silver Lobos first: while they're up he uses Net, which Stops a character.
- Once they're gone he switches to a double-strength Charge. Keep HP up.
- Locke can steal a **Mythril Knife** from him, a good early upgrade.

### Monsters here
[[Wererat]] · [[Bandit]] · [[Spritzer]] · [[Megalodoth]] · [[Silver Lobo]]

### Don't miss
- **Mog's party:** have Mog fight a battle here so he learns his first dance. Locke can also steal Mog's spear and shield before the Moogles leave, but you need Mog's group to win without them.
- Two chests in the west mine **improve later**: a Phoenix Down becomes a Guard Bracelet, and a Sleeping Bag becomes an Elixir. Leave them for the World of Ruin.
- The **classroom** south of the entrance has a free recovery spring and tutorial notes.

## Figaro Castle
@guide 4.4.1
@where Figaro Castle

Locke takes Terra to King Edgar of Figaro, a secret ally of the rebels. The Empire's court jester, Kefka, arrives right behind them.

### Where to go
- From Narshe, head south and west into the desert. The castle sits in the middle of it.
- Go to the throne room and talk to Edgar. Then explore: talk to the old woman in the west tower to name Edgar's brother, and return to Edgar.
- Kefka arrives. Play through the night scenes, then talk to the guard at the middle hall to set the escape in motion.
- After the fight you're on chocobos. Head southeast to the cave.

### Boss: [[Magitek Armor]] ×2
- It's a back attack: switch rows first.
- Edgar's tools do the work: Auto Crossbow hits both, Noiseblaster Confuses them. They're immune to Poison, so skip the Bioblaster.

### Monsters here
[[Leaf Bunny]] · [[Darkwind]] · [[Sand Ray]] · [[Alacran]]

### Side trips
- The castle shops sell Edgar's **Noiseblaster** and **Bioblaster**. You get an Auto Crossbow free, so don't buy one.
- The west wing inn is free.

## South Figaro and Mt. Kolts
@guide 4.6.1
@where Figaro Cave, South Figaro, Sabin's House, Mt. Kolts

Edgar's twin brother Sabin left the throne behind to train under the martial artist Duncan. Something has gone wrong at Duncan's place, and the trail leads into the mountains.

### Where to go
- Go through the cave southeast of the castle (a recovery spring is on the first screen) to reach South Figaro.
- Shop in South Figaro, then go north to Duncan's cabin (free bed) and on to Mt. Kolts.
- Climb the mountain west to east, weaving in and out of caves. The path on is to the northwest of the big central cave.
- Save at the point before the long bridge, then follow the figure you keep seeing to the boss.
- After the fight, Sabin joins. The exit is just past a cave with a Tent.

### Boss: [[Vargas|Vargas (boss)]]
- First defeat his two [[Ipooh]] bears; he can't be targeted until they fall.
- Then deal about 720 damage. Sabin arrives, Vargas blows the others away and puts a doom countdown on Sabin.
- Use the Blitz **Raging Fist** (←, →, ←, then confirm) once. That ends the fight immediately.

### Monsters here
[[Urok]] · [[Foper]] · [[Hornet]] · [[Zaghrem]] · [[Gorgias]] · [[Cirpius]] · [[Trillium]] · [[Ipooh]]

### Don't miss
- **Name Shadow:** a man in black heads into the South Figaro café when you arrive. Talk to him there; this is your only chance to name him.
- In the central cave of Mt. Kolts, go south around the plateau for the **Main Gauche** (Locke), and hug the wall to a hidden path northeast for the **Gigas Glove**.

### Side trips
- South Figaro's shops are a big upgrade: Great Sword, Kenpo Gi, Heavy Shield, Plumed Hat, and relics. Star Pendants and Jeweled Rings help on the mountain (Poison and Petrify).
- The mansion's secret passage and first basement can be explored now for a **Hyper Wrist** and **Hermes Sandals** (use the password "Courage" with the old man), though they're part of Locke's later story too.
- Chests in the cave and town change later; a few are worth waiting for (see the Figaro Cave notes in Locke's chapter).

### Good time to grind
- The forests around South Figaro give more EXP than the plains, and Duncan's cabin is a free bed nearby.

## The Returners and the Lethe River
@guide 4.9.1
@where Returners' Hideout & Lete River, Lethe River

The Returners, the rebel group led by Banon, want Terra's help. Then the Empire hits South Figaro, and everyone has to scatter.

### Where to go
- Follow the strip north from Mt. Kolts to the Returners' Hideout.
- After meeting Banon, you control Terra. Talk to everyone (Locke, Sabin, Edgar), then to the guard at the entrance, then to Banon outside.
- When asked to be the rebels' hope, answer as you like (see Don't miss). Locke leaves; Banon joins.
- Go south to the river. Once you're on the raft there's no menu, so equip first.
- Pick routes at the forks; all paths reach the save points. The boss waits after the second save point.

### Boss: [[Ultros]]
- Banon must survive: if he falls, it's game over. Have him use **Pray** (his healing command) whenever anyone is hurt.
- Ultros is weak to Fire and Lightning, but counters Fire with Ink. Edgar's Auto Crossbow and Sabin's **Aura Cannon** do steady damage.
- 3,000 HP, so it's a long fight. Keep everyone topped up; his Tentacle hits hard.

### Monsters here
[[Lesser Lopros]] · [[Nautiloid]] · [[Exocite]]

### Don't miss
- **Genji Glove or Gauntlet:** saying "no" to Banon three times gets you the Genji Glove; "yes" gives the Gauntlet. Or say no once, then talk to the guard in the north cave for the Genji Glove and still say yes afterwards. The Genji Glove (two weapons) is the more useful of the two.
- In the hideout, a hidden path behind the crates in the north leads to a **White Cape**. The chests also hold an **Air Knife** and a **Knight's Code**.

### Good time to grind
- The Lethe River has a loop: at the second fork, "Up" circles back to the same fork, with fights on the way. With auto-battle on and Banon praying, it's an easy place to pile up levels.

## Choosing a scenario
@guide 4.11.1

The party splits three ways. You get a Moogle, a save point and three groups to pick from; all three stories must be finished, in any order.

### Where to go
- Talk to a group to start its story: **Locke** (South Figaro), **Terra, Edgar and Banon** (back to Narshe), or **Sabin** (a long trip east).
- Items are shared between scenarios, but equipment stays with whoever wears it. Unequip characters before their scenario ends if you want to pass gear on.

### Side trips
- A common order is Locke, then Terra, then Sabin. Sabin's story ends in Nikeah, with shops that can equip everyone for what comes next, and doing it last means more monsters for Gau to learn on the Veldt.

## Locke: escape from South Figaro
@guide 4.19.1
@where South Figaro, Cave To South Figaro

Locke is trapped in Imperial-occupied South Figaro. He needs disguises, a password, and a way out. He also finds an Imperial general in chains.

### Where to go
- Re-equip Locke first; he left his gear behind.
- Steal a disguise from a **Merchant** (one is in the item shop). The old man's grandson will then let you through.
- Steal the **Cider** from the merchant in the café cellar, give it to the old man, and pick the password **Courage**.
- Use the mansion's secret passage. In the cell you meet **Celes**; free her, and take the **clock key** from the guard.
- Wind the clock in the third room to open the basement maze, collect Celes's gear, and leave by the east path.
- Go back through the cave toward Figaro. The scenario ends as you leave it.

### Boss: [[Tunnel Armor]]
- Celes's **Runic** absorbs almost all its spells, including the Tek Laser it uses near the end. Let her Runic every turn while Locke attacks.
- The **Thunder Rod** from the cave chest casts a strong Thundara once when used as an item, enough to win outright.

### Monsters here
[[Merchant]] · [[Commander]] · [[Vector Hound]] · [[Gold Bear]] · [[Acrophies]] · [[Cartagra]]

### Don't miss
- Avoid the soldiers in **Heavy Armor** in town; they hit very hard. Losing just sends Locke back to the start.
- In the basement: **Iron Armor**, **Heavy Shield**, **Great Sword**, **Earring**, and, behind the wall south of the X-Potion, a hidden **Ribbon**. The secret room off the north-south hall has the **Hyper Wrist** and **Hermes Sandals** if you haven't taken them.
- **Cave chests change:** the upper chest is a Thunder Rod now but a **Hero's Ring** in the World of Ruin. Your call.

## Terra: back to Narshe
@guide 4.12.1
@where Narshe, Lete River

Terra, Edgar and Banon finish the river trip and sneak back into Narshe through the mines.

### Where to go
- Finish the raft ride, then walk to Narshe. The guards won't let Terra in.
- Rest at the classroom spring, then check the wall west of the town entrance for the secret mine path.
- In the far west mine, a light traces a path across one room. Follow it exactly to pass. Straying starts a fight.
- Continue east through the mines to the old man's house to finish.

### Monsters here
[[Wererat]] · [[Bandit]] · [[Valeor]] · [[Wild Rat]] · [[Darkside]] · [[Spectre|Specter]] · [[Eukaryote]]

### Don't miss
- **Rare Rages:** stepping off the light path is the only place to meet [[Darkside]], [[Specter]] and [[Eukaryote]]. Fight them at least once so Gau can learn them later.
- The **Moogle cave** north of where Terra fell has a chest: a Rune Blade now, or a **Ribbon** if you wait for the World of Ruin.
- Unequip Terra and Edgar before entering the old man's house if others need their gear.

## Sabin: Shadow and the Imperial camp
@guide 4.13.1
@where Imperial Camp, Doma Castle

Sabin washes up far to the east. With the ninja Shadow, he finds the Empire besieging the castle of Doma, and Kefka about to do something unforgivable.

### Where to go
- Visit the house to the east. Shadow offers to join (he may leave after any battle until you board the train later). A traveling merchant outside sells Shuriken for Shadow's Throw.
- Head west then south to the Imperial camp. Scenes switch between the camp and Doma's knight Cyan.
- Explore the camp, chase Kefka through it, and fight his guards. After the poisoning scene, Cyan joins and you escape in Magitek armor.
- Leave the camp (you can't come back) and head south to the forest.

### Monsters here
[[Aepyornis]] · [[Chippirabbit]] · [[Nettlehopper]] · [[Stray Cat]] · [[Soldier]] · [[Templar]] · [[Soldier]] · [[Cadet]] · [[Magitek Armor]]

### Don't miss
- **Satellite:** the left chest in the tent northwest of the second Kefka meeting is guarded by a [[Satellite]], found nowhere else. It has 1,800 HP and counters Blitzes with Berserk; Shadow's Shuriken and plain attacks are safest. Prize: **Green Beret**. The right chest has a **Mythril Glove**.
- **Dobermans:** in the east tent, choose to *kick* the chest to meet [[Doberman]]s, also unique to this camp.
- A hidden **Barrier Ring**: walk behind the Satellite tent to the dead end.

## Sabin: the Phantom Train
@guide 4.14.1
@where Phantom Forest & Phantom Train, Phantom Train

South of Doma, the Phantom Forest hides a train that carries the dead to the next world. The party boards it by mistake.

### Where to go
- Cross the forest (a recovery spring is on the lake screen; northeast at the crossroads). Shadow can still leave here.
- On the train, visit the caboose first (save point), then work forward car by car.
- In the seventh car you get trapped: climb out, jump forward, and pull the switch in the sixth car to cut loose the rear cars.
- The dining car is a free rest stop. Continue to the locomotive.
- In the engine, open the first and third valves, then pull the switch by the smokestack to start the boss.

### Boss: [[Phantom Train]]
- It's a back attack; put everyone in the back row.
- Use your strongest moves. Sabin can **Meteor Strike** (suplex) the train, and it's very effective.
- Its Evil Toot causes random ailments; keep a few cures ready. 1,900 HP goes fast with Shadow throwing.

### Monsters here
[[Ghost]] · [[Poplium]] · [[Cloud]] · [[Angel Whisper]] · [[Living Dead]] · [[Bomb]] · [[Oversoul]] · [[Specter]]

### Don't miss
- **Ghost party members:** some ghosts join you and can **Possess** one enemy for an instant kill. Others sell items, including Shuriken.
- **Third car, west room:** three chests with a **Sniper Eye** and Phoenix Downs, and a monster-in-a-box [[Specter]] that drops a **Hyper Wrist**. It's tough; a ghost's Possess wins instantly.
- **Earring** in the dining car (enter from the west side, outside).
- [[Oversoul]] only appears in the sixth car. Fight one if you want its Rage later.
- [[Siegfried]] jumps you in the fourth car and takes the chest, but he's all bluster.

## Sabin: Gau, the Veldt and Nikeah
@guide 4.16.1
@where Baren Falls, Mobliz, Crescent Mountain, Serpent Trench, Nikeah

Off the train, the party jumps a waterfall onto the Veldt, home to monsters from everywhere and a wild child named Gau.

### Where to go
- At Baren Falls, jump. After a string of fish fights, the boss appears.
- Wash up on the Veldt. Go east to **Mobliz** and buy **Dried Meat**.
- Fight on the Veldt until Gau shows up after a battle, and use the Dried Meat on him. He joins.
- Go south to Crescent Mountain. The diving helmet is in the southeast; taking it starts the Serpent Trench (no return for a while).
- In the trench, choose right at both forks for treasure. You surface at Nikeah.
- Shop in Nikeah, then talk to the ship's captain to end the scenario.

### Boss: [[Rhizopas]]
- Kill it quickly; every other turn it can use El Nino, a heavy water attack. Don't try to suplex it.

### Monsters here
[[Opinicus Fish]] · [[Actinian]] · [[Anguiform]] · [[Aspiran]]

### Don't miss
- **How Gau learns Rages:** in a Veldt battle, use his **Leap**. He leaves; keep fighting on the Veldt with three people until he returns. He learns the Rages of the monsters in both battles. Any monster you've fought anywhere can show up here.
- **Mobliz letters:** read the wounded soldier's letter, then pay the post office 500 gil to reply for him, five times in all (rest at the inn between). The reward is a **Tintinnabulum**.
- **Serpent Trench:** right at the first fork leads to an X-Potion; right at the second leads to a drained-pool cave with a **Green Beret**.
- Aspirans counter special attacks with Giga Volt. Use plain attacks, or Gau's Belmodar Rage, which absorbs it.

### Side trips
- **Mobliz** sells the Magus Hat (good for Terra and Celes), Kotetsu for Cyan and Mythril Claws for Sabin. You can sleep free in the back of the relic shop.
- **Nikeah** is the best shopping so far (Silk Robes, relics, Smoke Bombs). An Elixir hides in the inn's clock.

### Good time to grind
- Veldt battles give gil but **no EXP**. For levels, fight around Nikeah before boarding the ship.

## The battle on the Narshe snowfield
@guide 4.21.1
@where Narshe, Narshe Cliffs

All three stories meet in Narshe. The Empire, led by Kefka, comes for the frozen esper on the cliffs, and everyone has to hold the line.

### Where to go
- On the cliff path, talk to each waiting character to swap them in and **update everyone's equipment**. Save.
- Tell Banon you're ready, then form three parties. One strong party, or a 3–2–2 split with Terra and Celes apart for healing, both work.
- Stop soldiers before any reach Banon. Defeat the rider guarding Kefka, then Kefka himself, to win.
- Afterward, Terra is gone. Pick a party of up to four and explore Narshe freely.

### Boss: [[Hell's Rider]]
- Weak to Fire and Poison (Bioblaster). His SilverPike hits very hard; he counters Steal with it, but he carries a **Mythril Vest**.
- Sabin's Meteor Strike works and isn't countered.

### Boss: [[Kefka]]
- 3,000 HP and lots of magic (Blizzara is the dangerous one). Celes's **Runic** soaks his spells.
- Physical attacks do much better than magic: Raging Fist, Cyan's Fang, Auto Crossbow.
- You win a **Peace Ring**. Locke can steal an **Ether**.

### Monsters here
[[Corporal]] · [[Hunting Hound]] · [[Fidor]] · [[Heavy Armor]]

### Don't miss
- The old man's house (south of the one Terra woke in) now lets you take everything: **5,000 gil, Earring, Thief's Knife, Reflect Ring, Thief's Bracer, Hyper Wrist**.
- Elixirs in the clocks of Arvis's house and the elder's house.

### Side trips
- Narshe's shops add the **Mythril Shield** (the best shield you can buy for a while), Silk Robes and Moonring Blade.
- The weapon shop's back room is a free bed.

## Kohlingen, Jidoor and Zozo
@guide 4.23.1
@where Kohlingen, Jidoor, Mt. Zozo, Zozo

Terra flew off in a burst of light. The trail leads south, through towns she was seen passing, to Zozo: a city of thieves and liars.

### Where to go
- Pick a party of up to four. You'll be away from Narshe for a while, so bring a healer (Celes).
- Go south to Figaro Castle. In the basement, ask the old man to take the castle under the sand to the **Kohlingen** side.
- Kohlingen points you south to **Jidoor**, a long walk down the coast; there's a chocobo stable just past the cabin north of Kohlingen if you'd rather ride.
- In Jidoor, people say Terra flew north into the mountains. Follow the east side of the range north to **Zozo**.
- Climb the tower with the Relics sign. You'll step out of windows and across to the next building; the man outside the inn warns "don't jump between buildings", which is the hint that you can.
- Beat the boss at the top and talk to the light by Terra's bed.

### Boss: [[Dadaluma]]
- Physical attacks work better than magic; he raises Protect after losing about 1,350 HP.
- He calls two [[Iron Fist]]s as time passes, so hit hard early.
- Worth stealing from: a **Thief's Bracer** is possible, and he gives back what he steals from you.

### Monsters here
Kohlingen area: [[Fossil Dragon]] · [[Vulture]] · [[Bloodfang]] · [[Paraladia]] · [[Iron Fist]] · [[Rock Wasp]] · [[Sand Ray]] · [[Alacran]]

Zozo: [[Hill Gigas]] · [[Gobbledygook]] · [[Harvester]] · [[Veil Dancer]]

### Don't miss
- At the top of Zozo you receive your first **espers**: Ramuh, Kirin, Siren and Cait Sith. See the Party page's "How espers work" for how to use them.
- **Zozo treasures:** Burning Fist (Sabin) on an outside door near the top, Brigand's Glove on a doorway above the crane, Hermes Sandals and an X-Potion beside Terra's bed.
- **Chainsaw** for Edgar: set the clock in Zozo's inn to **6:10:50**.
- The [[Fossil Dragon]] in the Kohlingen desert is far tougher than anything nearby (it can Zombie a character). Running is fine.

### Side trips
- **Kohlingen:** with Locke in the party, visit the northeast house's basement and Rachel's house for his backstory. A Green Beret hides behind the statue in the northeast house (enter from the north).
- **Cabin north of Kohlingen:** a **Hero's Ring** in the lower-left pot.
- Bring **Edgar as party leader** to Figaro Castle for half-price shopping, including the Flash and Drill tools.
- **Jidoor** sells Ninja Gear, the White Dress, Kiku-ichimonji (Cyan), Kaiser Knuckle (Sabin) and Earrings.
- Shadow can be hired at Kohlingen's inn for 3,000 gil if you have a free slot, but he may leave after any fight.

### Good time to grind
- The forests between Kohlingen and Jidoor pay well. Zozo's monsters give good EXP but hit hard; the [[Hill Gigas]] can Magnitude 8 the party as it dies.

## The opera house
@guide 4.27.1
@where Opera House, Jidoor

To reach the Empire's capital you need an airship, and the only one belongs to a gambler who plans to kidnap the opera's star singer. Celes looks just like her.

### Where to go
- Go back to Jidoor and into Owzer's mansion. The opera's impresario is there with a letter from Setzer; follow him.
- Go to the opera house south of Jidoor and talk to the impresario.
- As Celes, read the score in the dressing room, then go on stage. Choose the lyrics in the order the score gives, dance with the prince three times when he stops, then take the flowers up to the balcony before time runs out.
- As Locke, find Ultros's note and report it. You then have **five minutes** to reach Ultros above the stage: in the upper-right room press the **far-right switch**, then go through the door across the balcony and through the rafters.
- After the fight, deal with Setzer. The airship flies you south.

### Boss: [[Ultros]]
- He moves between four spots on stage; each is a fresh 2,550 HP, so focus on finishing one spot.
- Weak to Fire and Lightning: Fira and Thundara from your new espers, or Ramuh's summon, work well. Siren's Slow helps.
- He counters Blitzes and Bushido with an attack that causes Sap. Bring Green Cherries in case he sings Imp.

### Monsters here
[[Goetia]] · [[Stunner]]

### Don't miss
- Celes takes all her gear off (relics and esper too) before going on stage. Hand it to the others for the Ultros fight, and re-equip her afterwards.
- The other switches in the upper room trigger funny scenes. They waste time, so try them on a save you can reload.
- In the rafters, kill the yellow [[Goetia]] first; killing all the grey ones just brings more.

## The southern continent
@guide 4.30.1
@where Albrook, Tzen, Maranda, Vector

The airship lands near Albrook on the Empire's own continent. Your target is the Magitek Research Facility in Vector, but the towns around it have useful gear.

### Where to go
- Re-equip Celes. The airship has a free bed and a shop, and a man below deck can strip gear from the party members back in Narshe.
- Albrook is next to the landing spot. Vector, your goal, is to the north.
- Tzen (north, past Vector) and Maranda (far west) are optional detours; there's a chocobo stable in the forest east of Tzen and another east of Maranda.

### Monsters here
Plains: [[Joker]] · [[Don]] · [[Wyvern]] · [[Grasswyrm]]

Forests: [[Litwor Chicken]] · [[Wyvern]] · [[Don]]

Deserts: [[Fossil Dragon]] · [[Bug]]

### Don't miss
- **Albrook:** an Elixir in the clock between the café and relic shop. The relic shop sells the **Reflect Ring** and **Amulet**; the item shop has Remedies and Teleport Stones.
- **Tzen** sells the Boomerang (Locke, from the back row) and **Hermes Sandals**.
- **Maranda** sells **Mythril Mail**, a big armor upgrade.

### Side trips
- The Imperial Base east of Albrook only throws you out for now. Come back after the banquet.

### Good time to grind
- **Forests** pay the most EXP, gil and AP here. [[Wyvern]]s (484 EXP, weak to Ice) and [[Litwor Chicken]]s are good targets; don't leave a Wyvern alone or it uses Cyclonic, and don't leave a ChickenLip alone or it casts Quake.
- The desert's [[Fossil Dragon]]s give more EXP but hit hard.
- If you're holding off, the next dungeon's [[Flan]]s give big EXP for very little HP.

## Vector and the Magitek Factory
@guide 4.31.1
@where Vector, Magitek Factory

The imperial capital. The guards in the north of town attack on sight, so slip into the factory that makes the Empire's armor.

### Where to go
- In Vector, avoid the north. The free inn may rob you; instead, visit the old lady in the hut to the west and refuse to pledge to the Empire. Beat her two guards, and she heals you free from then on.
- In the southeast, an old man behind crates distracts the guards. Climb the left crate, follow the girders, and walk the train tracks north into the factory.
- The factory is a maze of conveyor belts, pipes and cranes. Explore it for treasure, then ride the east conveyor to watch Kefka dump Ifrit and Shiva below. Follow them.
- Save in the room west of Ifrit, then talk to him.

### Boss: [[Ifrit]] and [[Shiva]]
- Each is weak only to the other's element: Ice on Ifrit, Fire on Shiva. Physical attacks barely scratch them unless they ignore defense (Edgar's **Drill** or **Chainsaw**).
- Ifrit is the dangerous one (Fira, a fire breath, and Firaga if you hit him with magic three times). Beat either one to win.
- Afterward, talk to both again to get **Ifrit** (Strength +1 at level-up) and **Shiva** as espers.

### Monsters here
[[Sergeant]] · [[Garm]] · [[Proto Armor]] · [[Onion Knight]] · [[Flan]] · [[General]] · [[Trapper]]

### Don't miss
- **Factory treasure:** **Flametongue**, **Thunder Blade** and **Icebrand** (through the pipe behind the crates in the northeast of the south area, along with a **Zephyr Cloak**), plus **Golden Armor**, **Golden Helm**, **Golden Shield** and **Dragoon Boots** in side rooms. Get them before following Kefka, because you can't come back down.
- Everything here shrugs off physical attacks. Use magic (Thunder especially), Sabin's Aura Cannon, Cyan's Bushido and Edgar's tools.
- [[Trapper]]s use Lv. 5 Death: anyone whose level is a multiple of 5 dies instantly. Kill them first.
- **Don't fight the [[Guardian]]** at the palace gate. It can't be beaten yet; run.

### Good time to grind
- [[Flan]]s in the room with Ifrit and Shiva: 788 EXP each for only 255 HP, and they're weak to Fire. A lone Flan calls in more Flans, which means more EXP. You can also steal Magicite Shards from them.

## The Magitek Research Facility
@guide 4.31.5

[[Lenergia]] · [[Destroyer]] · [[Magna Roader]] (purple and red)

## The airship and Narshe
@guide 4.33.1
@where Narshe, Jidoor, Tzen, Veldt, Thamasa

With the Blackjack yours, the Returners regroup in Narshe and plan to reach the espers' world through the Sealed Gate. Before going, there's a lot you can do.

### Where to go
- Fly to Narshe. The Returners set the next goal: the Imperial Base and the cave to the Sealed Gate, east of Albrook.
- Change your party by talking to people on the airship.

### Don't miss
- **Lone Wolf and Mog:** talk to the old man in southeast Narshe, then catch the thief Lone Wolf at the old man's house. Follow him north through the mines (east along the tracks) to the cliffs. ### Good time to grind

## The cave to the Sealed Gate
@guide 4.36.1
@where Imperial Base, Cave To The Sealed Gate

Terra plans to call the espers through the Sealed Gate and ask for their help. The Imperial Base guarding it has been strangely abandoned.

### Where to go
- Bring **Terra** (required) and a balanced party with healing and a range of spells.
- Fly east of Albrook to the Imperial Base. Walk through it and cross the bridge to the cave.
- **Basement 2:** the bridges flip between two layouts. Stand only on tiles that exist in both, and wait for the flip.
- **The lava cavern:** press the switch on the trap-looking bridge (you drop down, but a bridge to a **Genji Glove** appears). On the plateau, press the **left** switch first (a save point and Tent); the right switch summons an optional [[Ninja]].
- Keep east, pressing bridge switches as you go, to the Sealed Gate. Kefka is waiting.
- Afterwards, walk back out through the base. The airship is attacked, and you land southeast of Maranda. Head to Vector.

### Monsters here
[[Provoker]] · [[Lich]] · [[Outcast]] · [[Antares]] · [[Zombie Dragon]] · [[Ninja]]

### Don't miss
- **Ultima Weapon** (sword) in the treasure room south and west of the switches near the Elixir chest, along with two Magicite Shards. It's weak now but grows with your level, and it ignores defense.
- Hidden treasures under the stair bridge: from its bottom tile, one step down and one left (Invisibility Scroll); then six steps south; then four up and four right. Search where you stand, not facing a wall.
- **Assassin's Dagger:** don't use it in this cave. Its instant kill fully heals undead, and almost everything here is undead.
- [[Outcast]]s drain huge amounts of HP, but only as much as they've lost. Kill them one at a time, right after they use Lifeshaver.
- [[Provoker]]s counter with Imp Song; one-shot them with Ice or Holy.

## The banquet
@guide 4.38.1
@where Vector, Imperial Palace

The espers' rampage has broken the Empire, and Emperor Gestahl invites the Returners to a peace banquet. How you behave decides what you get from it.

### Where to go
- Walk to Vector (or ride from the chocobo stable near where you land). Meet Banon and Arvis at the palace gate.
- Inside, Gestahl gives you **four minutes** to talk to his soldiers before the banquet. Each soldier you talk to scores a point; four of them attack, and beating them scores more. Sprint Shoes help.
- Then answer the questions at the banquet. Be polite, don't repeat a question to Gestahl, and remember which question you asked him first.
- Afterwards, only Terra and Locke continue. They're to go to Albrook's docks.

### Don't miss
- **Score well for the rewards:** a high score unlocks the Imperial Base's treasure room, and your answers decide whether Doma and the southern towns are freed. Hitting the target:
  - Talk to everyone: start outside, go up the west side of the palace entering every door (the toilet and the roof's device room count), then come down the east side.
  - The soldiers in the throne room don't count.
  - Kill the attackers in one hit with Thundara: the three [[Sergeant]] fights and the [[Mega Armor]].
  - Visit Kefka in the jail afterwards; it doesn't score.
- **Imperial Base treasure room** (if unlocked): 41,000 gil, Angel Wings, Reflect Ring, Hermes Sandals, Alarm Earring, Angel Ring, Flametongue (in the stove), two Elixirs, an X-Potion and an Ether. Go before sailing.
- **Palace treasures:** Alarm Earring and X-Potion behind the lowest west door, Gale Hairpin in an east bedroom.

General Leo escorts Terra and Locke by ship to Thamasa, a village where the espers were last seen. The villagers clearly know more than they say.

### Where to go

### Monsters here
[[Chimera]] · [[Briareus]] · [[Devourer]] · [[Balloon]]

### Don't miss
- **Memento Ring:** after first meeting Strago, go back into his house, upstairs, and search the wall left of the small table.
- Strago knows no spells yet. Give him an esper straight away.
- Shadow leaves after this dungeon. Take back his esper and anything you want first.
- [[Chimera]]s in the forests are much tougher than the plains monsters (2,237 HP).

## Before the Floating Continent

@where Doma Castle, Imperial Base, Veldt

The next trip is the last in this world. When it's over, everything changes, so this is the time to tidy up.

### Where to go

- When ready, fly to the Floating Continent from the airship's controls.

### Don't miss

Golem's **Stop** spell shuts it down; follow up with defense-ignoring attacks (Drill, Chainsaw, Cyan's Fang). Or just meet one and run, so Gau can learn its Rage on the Veldt.

### Good time to grind

## The Floating Continent
@guide 4.45.1
@where Floating Island, Floating Continent

Gestahl and Kefka have raised the espers' old home into the sky to reach the Warring Triad, three statues of godlike power. The Returners fly in to stop them.

### Where to go
- Pick **three** people. You'll fight six battles on the airship deck, then two bosses, with no rest in between.
- Land, save, and recruit Shadow.
- Paths appear as you walk toward them, and plateaus act as warps. Press the switches you find; one lowers ground to open a route back through an earlier section.
- Save at the plateau to the south near the end, then take the north plateau. Past the stairs you'll be asked whether to return to the airship. This is your last chance.
- After the big boss, Celes joins if she's not already with you. Then you have **six minutes** to escape.

### Boss: [[Ultros]] and [[Typhon]]
- Ultros is weak to Fire and Poison now. After about 4,200 damage he calls Typhon.
- Typhon is weak to Ice and Water and absorbs Fire. When it falls it sneezes you off the deck.

### Boss: [[Air Force]]
- Three targets. The body only attacks after the [[Missile Bay]] falls. Killing the [[Laser Gun]] summons a [[Bit]] that soaks up spells and starts a countdown to Wave Cannon.
- Simplest plan: leave the Laser Gun alone and hit the body with **Thundara**. Everything is weak to Lightning and Water.

### Boss: [[Ultima Weapon]]
- 24,000 HP and no weakness. Its normal attack and Flare hurt badly, and later it cycles big spells and a status attack.
- Bring your strongest attacks and heal constantly; Cura on everyone is your friend.

### Boss: [[Nelapa]]
- During the escape, has auto-Reflect. Use attacks that can't be reflected (Aqua Breath, Bushido, tools, physical hits). Weak to Ice, Lightning and Holy.

### Monsters here
Airship: [[Spitfire]] · [[Sky Armor]]

Continent: [[Apocrypha]] · [[Misfit]] · [[Brainpan]] · [[Behemoth]] · [[Ninja]] · [[Platinum Dragon]] · [[Dragon]] · [[Naude]]

### Don't miss
- **Wait for Shadow.** After Nelapa, don't jump to the airship until the timer reads **0:05**; Shadow arrives then. If you leave without him, he's gone for good.
- Blue orbs are chests: **Murasame** (Cyan), **Sasuke** (Shadow, guarded by [[Gigantos]], who is weak to Poison) and a **Beret**.
- Lores for Strago: **1000 Needles** from [[Brainpan]], Lv. 3 Confuse / Lv. 4 Flare / Lv. 5 Death from a lone [[Apocrypha]], **Doom** and **Roulette** from [[Nelapa]].
- Don't summon espers against [[Behemoth]]s; they counter with Meteor.

### Good time to grind
- Battles here give lots of **AP**. Before the last plateau, it's a great place to finish learning spells.

## Narshe, Mog and the Yeti
@guide 4.55.1
@where Narshe, Narshe Cliffs, Umaro's Cave

Narshe has been overrun by monsters. Somewhere in its mines, Mog is waiting, and something big lives in a cave beyond the cliffs.

### Where to go
- Bring **three** people so Mog can join on the spot.
- Use the secret entrance west of the town gate, follow the long path to the door in the southern rock, and go north through the old maze to the **Moogle cave**. Mog joins.
- Head north through the mines (east at the north mine entrance) to the snowy maze and the cliffs. Save at the point at the north end.
- At the cliff edge, fight the frozen esper. Beating it opens a path down to the Yeti's cave. Once you jump in, there's no save point and no way out until it's done.
- In the cave, the eastern switch on the far bridge drops you to the Yeti's lair. Take the bone carving's esper and win the fight; then talk to the Yeti **with Mog in the party** to recruit **Umaro**.

### Boss: [[Valigarmanda]]
- Encased in ice: it absorbs Ice and only **Fire** hurts it. It barely takes physical damage but is very weak to magic.

## The Tower of Fanatics
@guide 4.69.1
@where Tower of Fanatics

Kefka's cultists have built a tower to their god. Inside, only magic works: no Fight, no special commands, just spells and items.

### Where to go
- Prepare first:
  - **Reflect Rings** for everyone. Most enemies here cast spells, and with Reflect you bounce them back.
  - Cast **Float** before you go in (against Quake).
  - Bring Fire protection (Flame or Ice Shields) against Meltdown.
  - Bring your strongest Magic stats, and someone who knows **Reraise** (from Phoenix).
- Climb to the top: the floors repeat, with treasure rooms on the way. You can't Teleport out.
- The last chest at the top triggers the boss.

### Boss: [[Magic Master]]
- It changes its weakness every time you hit it. Either use non-elemental spells (Flare, Meteor, Ultima) or **don't attack at all**: with Reflect on, its own spells bounce back and it beats itself.
- **When it dies it casts Ultima**, about 6,000 damage to everyone. Cast **Reraise** on at least one person early or the whole climb is lost.
- Prize: the **Soul of Thamasa** (cast two spells per turn) and a Megalixir.

### Boss: [[Holy Dragon]] (optional)
- Roams the 26th-floor treasure room. It only casts Holy, which a Reflect Ring party bounces back. Drops the **Holy Lance**.

### Monsters here
Every "Magic" enemy is named for its level: L.10 to L.90. The L.60 casts Quake (stay Floating) and the L.90 casts Meteor, Flare and Meltdown. [[Magic Urn]]s are friendly and heal you; let them act.

### Don't miss
- **8th-floor room:** a Safety Bit, and search the wall right of the chest to open a room on the 7th floor with Edgar's best tool, the **Air Anchor**.
- **Genji Shield** (17th floor), **Kagenui** (Shadow's best weapon, 26th floor), **Force Armor** (35th floor).

### Good time to grind
- Battles here give **no EXP but lots of AP**: ideal for learning spells without levelling past esper bonuses.

@guide 4.72.1

The tower Kefka built from the ruins of the world. You'll storm it with **three parties** at once.

### Where to go
- Pick up to **twelve** fighters in three parties, each with a healer and both physical and magic damage. You can Teleport out at any time to rearrange.

### Boss: [[Inferno]]
- Three parts with crossing weaknesses (the body is weak to **Thunder**). While both arms live, it Petrifies someone every 30 seconds; Petrify protection or killing an arm stops that, but then it casts Meteor.

- **Goddess:** absorbs Thunder and Holy. Charm confuses for good, and she counters Fight. Use magic and special commands, and hit her from behind. Drops **Excalibur**.
- **Demon:** absorbs Fire and Wind. Stop followed by an instant kill, so Death protection helps. High magic evasion blocks most of its spells. Drops the **Radiant Lance**.

[[Vector Lythos]] · [[Primeval Dragon]] · [[Great Malboro]] · [[Great Behemoth]] · [[Prometheus]] · [[Gamma]] · [[InnoSent]] · [[Fortis]] · [[Duel Armor]] · [[Death Machine]] · [[Landworm]] · [[Outsider]] · [[Cherry]] · [[Yojimbo]] · [[Demon Knight]] · [[Dark Force]] · [[Muud Suud]] · [[Mover]] · [[Junk]] · [[Metal Hitman]] · [[Ahriman]] · [[Daedalus]] · [[Fiend Dragon]]

## The final battle
@guide 4.73.1
@where Kefka's Tower

Kefka waits at the top. You'll climb through three tiers of the gods' statue before reaching him.

### Where to go
- Put all twelve fighters in order. The first four start; anyone knocked out between tiers is replaced by the next in line. Spread gear so the backups are ready, and assign espers for their summons.
- There's no saving from here.

### Boss: tier 1 ([[Long Arm]], [[Visage]], [[Short Arm]])
- Kill them **front to back**: the Long Arm, then the Visage (Fire), then the Short Arm (Water). The others counter nastily if they die last.

### Boss: tier 2 ([[Power]], [[Machine]], [[Magic]], [[Tiger]])
- **Don't use Quake.** Silence on **Magic** shuts down its huge spell list. Machine is weak to Thunder, Tiger to Ice. Power hits ten times as it dies, so don't leave it to last.

### Boss: tier 3 ([[Rest]] and [[Lady]])
- **Kill Lady first**; she revives Rest. Use non-elemental attacks, since she absorbs every element.
- When Rest dies it may kill one or two people. Death protection helps.

### Boss: [[Kefka (final boss)|Kefka]]
- He opens with **Fallen One**, dropping everyone to 1 HP. Heal immediately (a Megalixir is perfect).
- After about 30,000 damage he starts double turns and counters with a huge spell. Below about 10,000 HP he counters with **Ultima**.
- His defenses are modest. Hit with your very best and keep everyone alive.

Then sit back and enjoy the ending. It changes depending on who you found in the World of Ruin.
